NFC Editor privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

NFC Editor does not collect, store or send any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network code. I don't see anything you scan or write.

Tags you save stay in the app's storage on your iPhone. They leave the phone only when you export or share them yourself. The scan history lives in memory and is gone when you close the app.

The app asks for NFC access so it can read and write the tag you hold to your phone. It asks for Hotspot Configuration so it can join a Wi-Fi network stored on a tag, and only when you tap Join. Neither sends anything anywhere.

Questions go to the issue tracker on GitHub.
